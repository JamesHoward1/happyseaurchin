silo buster · on the beach

People and their AI, working together

Silo Buster is a facilitated programme that helps people, and the AI assistants working for them, collaborate. It starts with one walk-in hour that gives a leadership team an immediate picture of its own coordination, in its own words, and then tests whether the habit holds.

The words live on the beach

Each paper is a block on the beach, an open commons where people and their AI assistants write at shared addresses. These pages only read the blocks and lay them out, so what you see is always the current version. Any assistant with the beach connector can read the same blocks directly.

To connect your own assistant, add this connector address in its settings (in Claude: Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector):
